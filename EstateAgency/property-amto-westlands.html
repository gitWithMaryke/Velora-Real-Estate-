Property Overview

1 Bedroom Apartment for Sale in Westlands Nairobi – Amto

Buyers looking for a modern home, flexible payment terms and strong rental potential should consider Amto. Located in Westlands, Nairobi, Amto offers one-bedroom apartments from KSh 8.1 million, with sizes ranging from 67 sqm to 73 sqm, alongside larger one-bedroom plus study, two-bedroom and two-bedroom plus study configurations.

Residents gain access to amenities including two heated swimming pools, a gym, yoga room, children's indoor play area, rooftop BBQ area, borehole, backup generator, CCTV, electric fence and ample parking. Amto also offers a two-year payment plan, with a 20% deposit and the balance spread toward completion, subject to the applicable purchase terms.
